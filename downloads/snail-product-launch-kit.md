# Original crochet pattern: Meadow Coil Snail

## Materials

- 3.25 mm crochet hook
- Worsted/aran yarn:
  - 55–65 g body color
  - 35–45 g shell color
  - Small amounts of dark brown or black for embroidery
  - Optional accent color for shell ridge and leaf
- Polyester fiberfill
- Yarn needle, stitch marker, scissors
- Optional: removable felt leaf, 8 cm square of felt, needle and thread

## Abbreviations (US terms)

`MR` magic ring · `sc` single crochet · `inc` 2 sc in one stitch · `dec` single crochet 2 together · `sl st` slip stitch · `ch` chain · `st/sts` stitch/stitches · `FO` fasten off

Numbers in parentheses are the stitch count at the end of the round. Repeat instructions between asterisks for the rest of the round.

## Tension and safety

Work firmly. If stuffing is visible, use a smaller hook. Stuff gradually so the fabric remains smooth rather than stretched. For a toy intended for a child under 3, embroider all facial details and omit detachable parts; otherwise follow your local toy-safety rules.

## 1. Body and head, worked as one piece

Use body color. Start at the nose and continue toward the tail.

1. MR, 6 sc (6)
2. inc around (12)
3. *sc, inc* around (18)
4. *2 sc, inc* around (24)
5–8. sc around (24)
9. *3 sc, inc* around (30)
10–14. sc around (30)
15. *3 sc, dec* around (24)
16. *2 sc, dec* around (18)

Begin stuffing the head firmly. Place a little extra filling at the cheeks.

17. *sc, dec* around (12)
18. dec around (6)

FO, leaving a 25 cm tail. Use the tail to close the opening, then weave it toward the back of the head.

### Lower body and tail

Attach body color at the underside of the head, about 4 stitches behind the nose. If preferred, use a long starting tail to sew the underside instead of attaching with a slip stitch.

1. 18 sc around the lower head edge (18)
2. *2 sc, inc* around (24)
3–8. sc around (24)
9. *2 sc, dec* around (18)
10–13. sc around (18)
14. *sc, dec* around (12)
15–18. sc around (12)
19. *sc, dec* around (8)
20–24. sc around (8)

Stuff the body lightly, keeping the underside flat. Continue with the tail:

25–30. sc around (8)
31. *2 sc, dec* around (6)
32–36. sc around (6)

Add the final stuffing before the tail becomes narrow. Flatten the last opening and sew it closed. Curve the tail gently upward as you weave in the end.

> **Shape check:** the head should be rounded, the middle should sit low and flat, and the tail should taper into a soft upward curl. If the body feels too narrow, add one or two rounds of sc before round 9.

## 2. Shell disk

Use shell color. This piece is a flat spiral disk that will be lightly stuffed and sewn to the body.

1. MR, 6 sc (6)
2. inc around (12)
3. *sc, inc* around (18)
4. *2 sc, inc* around (24)
5. *3 sc, inc* around (30)
6. *4 sc, inc* around (36)
7. *5 sc, inc* around (42)
8. *6 sc, inc* around (48)
9. *7 sc, inc* around (54)
10. sc around (54)
11. *7 sc, dec* around (48)
12. *6 sc, dec* around (42)

Add a thin layer of stuffing so the disk is domed, not overfilled.

13. *5 sc, dec* around (36)
14. *4 sc, dec* around (30)
15. *3 sc, dec* around (24)
16. *2 sc, dec* around (18)
17. *sc, dec* around (12)
18. dec around (6)

FO, close the opening, and leave a long sewing tail.

## 3. Shell spiral ridge

With an accent color, chain 42. Starting in the second chain from the hook, sl st in each chain to make a cord. Coil the cord into a loose spiral and sew it to the visible face of the shell. Begin at the center and stitch every 1–1.5 cm, keeping the spiral relaxed. Secure the outer end at the shell edge.

For a raised all-yarn ridge, replace the cord with a chain-42 surface slip-stitch line worked directly onto the shell. Do not pull tightly or the disk will cup.

## 4. Eye stalks, make 2

Use body color.

1. MR, 6 sc (6)
2–8. sc around (6)

Stuff very lightly. Flatten the opening and sew to the top-front of the head, spacing the stalks about 3 cm apart. Angle them slightly outward.

## 5. Eye tips, make 2

Use body color or a contrasting color.

1. MR, 6 sc (6)
2. inc around (12)
3. sc around (12)
4. *sc, dec* around (8)

Add a tiny amount of stuffing, FO, and sew closed. Attach one tip to each stalk. Embroider a small dark oval on each tip, or add a single white highlight stitch for a sleepy expression.

## 6. Optional leaf accessory

Use green yarn.

1. ch 9.
2. Starting in the second chain, sl st, sc, hdc, dc, dc, hdc, sc, sl st.
3. Turn. Work the same stitches along the opposite side of the chain, then sl st to the first stitch.

FO and weave in the end. Add a central vein with backstitch. Sew the leaf to the shell only if you want a permanent accessory. For a removable accessory, attach a small snap using strong thread and keep it away from children under 3.

## Assembly and finishing

1. Place the shell on the upper side of the body, slightly behind the head and centered over the widest section.
2. Pin through the shell edge at four points first, then add pins every 2–3 cm.
3. Sew around the entire shell edge with matching shell yarn. Take stitches through the body but avoid pulling hard enough to flatten it.
4. Add the spiral ridge.
5. Embroider a small curved smile and two short cheek marks. Keep the face on the front third of the head.
6. Hide all ends inside the body and check that no stuffing is visible.

### Colorway recipes

- **Moss + honey:** sage body, mustard shell, rust ridge, cream cheeks.
- **Blue hour:** dusty blue body, navy shell, lavender ridge, silver-gray cheeks.
- **Berry patch:** blush body, plum shell, coral ridge, cream cheeks.
- **Garden neutral:** oatmeal body, olive shell, terracotta ridge, dark brown face.
